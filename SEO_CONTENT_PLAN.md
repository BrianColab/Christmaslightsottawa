## Local SEO notes

- Keep copy focused on Ottawa and surrounding communities.
- Add real project photos with descriptive filenames and alt text.
- Add verified testimonials when approved.
- Keep service pages practical and specific to lower-level decorating.
- Avoid unconfirmed claims such as ratings, awards, warranties, or years in business.

## Schema notes

- Current schema uses LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, BlogPosting, and BreadcrumbList.
- Schema should continue using only confirmed facts.
- Do not add fake reviews, fake ratings, price ranges, business hours, street address, or social profiles.

## Google Business Profile recommendations

- Add final website URL.
- Confirm phone number matches the site.
- Add real lower-level decorating photos.
- Add service categories where available.
- Encourage real customer reviews after jobs are completed.
- Link GBP updates to seasonal services, quote reminders, takedown, and storage.
