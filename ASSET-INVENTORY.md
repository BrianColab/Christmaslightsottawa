# Christmas Lights Ottawa Asset Inventory

This is the current launch asset inventory for the new Christmas Lights Ottawa website.

## Images currently in the repo

- `assets/images/hero-lower-level-lighting.png` - original AI placeholder source for lower-level home lighting.
- `assets/images/porch-entry-lighting.png` - original AI placeholder source for porch, wreath, garland, and entryway decorating.
- `assets/images/garage-walkway-lighting.png` - original AI placeholder source for garage trim, shrubs, walkway accents, and pricing/blog visuals.
- `assets/images/commercial-storefront-lighting.png` - original AI placeholder source for commercial storefront decorating.
- `assets/images/optimized/*-1200.jpg` - compressed 1200px display images used for hero, Open Graph, article heroes, and larger responsive slots.
- `assets/images/optimized/*-640.jpg` - compressed 640px card/gallery images used through responsive `srcset`.

The optimized JPEGs are now used by the website. The larger PNG files are retained as editable/source placeholders until real client photography is provided.

## Logo and brand assets

- The current logo is an inline wreath mark in the HTML and server-rendered templates.
- No production logo file, favicon, brand guide, color sheet, or approved typography file is currently in the repo.

## Content assets that still need confirmation

- Testimonials are currently generic client-note placeholders and should be replaced with verified client reviews before launch.
- Trust claims such as years in business, insurance status, crew size, product warranties, and number of completed installs should be confirmed before being added back to visible copy.
- Gallery images are placeholders and should be replaced with real installs that show entryways, porches, railings, columns, garages, shrubs, walkways, wreaths, garlands, storefronts, and takedown/storage where possible.

## Production assets needed before launch

- Final Christmas Lights Ottawa logo, favicon, and social sharing image.
- 8 to 15 real installation photos focused on lower-level decorating.
- At least 3 commercial storefront photos if commercial decorating will be promoted.
- Verified testimonials with names or approved attribution.
